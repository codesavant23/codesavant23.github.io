Who I was

Hi, I'm Filippo Mecozzi, the author of the portfolio containing this life testimony. I'm a 25-year-old Italian man who, despite his deep passion for computers, programming, and dedication to studying in the IT field, following various negative events, made profoundly wrong life choices.

High-school

Particularly around the age of 15, after having already had a troubled past with my peers, I began, like many of my age group, smoking cannabis out of curiosity and was immediately hooked, so much so that it became one of my regular weekend pastimes.
After a very short time i also started smoking cigarettes.

Quickly, over the course of a few years, my friendships suddenly changed, and at a certain point I even found myself hanging out with people involved in crime and misdemeanors to get the cannabis.

I thought I was happy, but in reality I was melancholic, empty, sometimes depressed, sometimes even apathic, and angry over many of the events that happened with those people.

Even though I continued my studies in computer science and my passion was still there (fading and rekindling) my life was slowly falling apart.

Then I graduated as an IT technician and started university not so much later.

God in my life

My relationship with God wasn't as straightforward as it might have been for others. As a child, I was a Catholic Christian who believed in Jesus and attended church at my parents' urging. But after some difficult times in middle school, I distanced myself from God, unable to explain why so much harm could happen if He loves us and if someone didn't want to be a bad/harmful person.

Consequently I began to believe that Christianity was wrong, false, or incomplete. This led me to atheism for a time, then I reconsidered the existence of God but began to blaspheme and blame Him for many things (almost anything).

Everything got worse and worse, and around the time I started smoking cannabis, I began to try to explain the existence of life and its purpose in many other ways.
I became interested in other religions/theories of life: pure evolutionism, Rastafarianism, Buddhism, theories of reincarnation, and many others.

I also became interested in the occult, probably out of a curiosity I've always had.
Now I can thank God I never done any ritual or "game" that i remember involving the occult due to the fear, mixed to the curiosity, i always had on such things.
