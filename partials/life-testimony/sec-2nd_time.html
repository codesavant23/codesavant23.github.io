The Second Time

One Addiction Is Gone

One evening, Elon, some of his friends I had recently met, and I were smoking when an old man approached us. After talking to Elon' friends for a while, he discovered that I was also a Christian and told me that he had the same problem I had, but with alcohol.
He told me that what I was doing was wrong because I was polluting what God had given me: my body and my mental faculties.
And a verse that says "The body is the temple of God" (1 Corinthians 3:16) resonated in my mind.

That was the first time in my life that I consciously decided to quit cannabis forever.

My battle began: at first, I tried to reduce the amount I smoked each day. But something kept pulling me back, even just one step (or one puff), toward my previous state.

But thanks to God's help (I realized this later), in about 5-7 days I completely stopped smoking cannabis, forever, after about 10 consecutive years of use.

Then God gave me the opportunity, one night, to throw away and destroy my stash completely: I went to a field, threw away everything that was left, and poured a small bottle of water over it so it would be ruined and impossible to smoke by anyone.
If I remember correctly, the next day there was a downpour in my city, in the middle of summer, and the day after that it was even hotter than usual: the Lord also contributed with His help, ruining it even more.

Meanwhile, my relationship with Elon began to decline, especially when I threw away my stuff (not his): he was annoyed by it.

Cigarettes

Of course, I was then led to think: «If cannabis pollutes God’s temple, cigarettes do too.»
So I decided to try to quit smoking cigarettes as well.

It was too difficult, impossible, since I couldn't go two days without a cigarette. The thought tormented me, maybe not immediately in the morning, but already by lunchtime or after lunch.

I tried everything to quit: I changed the place where I kept the tobacco (so it wouldn't be within reach) — I didn't throw it away because I knew I would buy it again — I tried reducing the daily amount or spreading it out throughout the day (which consisted of 1-2 cigarettes), I tried not smoking at all, I tried distracting my mind with other things... but nothing worked. I simply couldn't do it!

Those weeks were really hard and dark. I cried a lot, I was discouraged, and I thought, «God, I want to do Your will, and I'm trying... but I can't.»

During all this time, the coincidences continued, and God was always inventing new ways to make me understand that everything was in His hands. One day, I was crying while sitting on a small bin on the balcony, and He drew my attention to a petal that had fallen about a meter away from me. With a gust of wind, He brought it closer to me, lifted it up, brushed my nose with it, made it do a loop, and then caused it to land precisely in the wastepaper basket next to me on my left.

After a few weeks had passed without any noticeable progress, one evening, as often happened during those months, I was video calling a friend who was in Turin for his studies (fictional name: Elijah). Elijah and I had been reading Bible passages together for a while. We had met a few years earlier through a childhood friend of mine, a mutual dear friend for both, and compared to my other ones, Elijah was a calm, level-headed person.

He had recently become a Christian, and his conversion amazed me. That evening, he asked me if I wanted to come up to Turin and stay with him for a few days. I readily accepted. However, he told me to check the trains and see how much they cost, but something was strongly pushing me to stay firm in my choice.

The next day, shortly after getting up, I planned the trip: from September 5th to 8th.

The day before, or a few days before I left, I had a special call with Elijah, during which he explained his experience with God to me: he had been like me, unable to quit smoking cigarettes, and it hurt him, but he couldn't stop no matter how hard he tried. Then, one day, he received the baptism in the Holy Spirit and no longer needed to smoke; the "emptiness" he felt had been filled.

I was perplexed: «But... what do you mean by ‘Baptism in the Holy Spirit’?» Despite this, something intrigued me and made me believe that what he was saying was true.
Elijah also clarified that he was not Catholic; he was Christian, and he trusted and believed only what was written in the Word of God.

Turin

On September 5th, I left for Turin and decided to try something by entrusting my father with the tobacco before leaving, asking him to hide it so I would have to search for it all over the house when I got back. I also thought, «Who knows how long it will be before I buy a pack of cigarettes or tobacco in Turin?... Probably not long.»

Anyway, I took the train, arrived in Turin, and as soon as I got there, while leaving the station and waiting for Elijah, I noticed that I was frequently zoning out. This is typical of cannabis smokers, but I had quit months earlier, and on other occasions when I had quit, nothing like this had ever happened. Before arriving in Turin that day, despite my sadness and discouragement, it had never happened either at that level.

The zoning out was too frequent and too intense to be normal: about every 5-10 seconds, maybe even less, I would zone out, and almost immediately I would feel like I was at its peak, submerged in my thoughts. I didn't understand what was happening, I was a little scared, and it was difficult for me to follow any conversation for more than a few seconds.

Elijah arrived shortly after, took me to his house, and had me settle into his room on the air mattress he had prepared for me.
It was late afternoon when I arrived, and shortly afterward we had dinner. Later, as we were talking, he told me that a brother in Christ had given him some music tapes that he kept in a soft cooler bag. He had also given him a tape recorder to listen to them on.

These tapes, however, were special: they did not contain recorded music, but worship services to God and testimonies from people whose lives had been changed by Jesus Christ.

Elijah took the tapes, spread them out on his bed, and said to me, "Come on! Pick one."
I didn't know which one to choose, both because of the constant zoning out and because they all looked the same.

So I thought something like, «Lord, I know You hear my thoughts. Please let me choose the tape You want, the one You know.»
Immediately afterward, my eyes fixed on one tape. I picked it up, and when I looked at the date, I recognized the sign the Lord used with me: 23.

On the tape, there were some hymns to God that deeply touched me; they were very pleasant, which was strange because lately I had not been able to stop listening to psy-trance music (it was like another addiction).
Then came the testimony of a young woman: the Lord had saved her, if I remember correctly, from multiple tumors. He had also made her husband stop gambling and later converted him to Christ. She said that nothing was impossible for God.
I was deeply touched by this.

September 6th, 2025

September 6th was the day I would remember for the rest of my life.

I remember going to lunch with Elijah and some of his friends, including a brother in faith, and I had the opportunity to evangelize one of his friends who, by pure coincidence (ironically, because nothing is a coincidence), attended the same university as me in Udine, even though I had never met him. He was studying computer science like me, and we had Elijah as a mutual friend. I was deeply moved by the fact that, after telling him about my experience on October 31, 2022, I felt that presence/force again, although very slightly, in my fingertips. I told Elijah about it too.

I don't remember how we spent the afternoon (also because of the constant zoning out that would not leave me alone). Evening came, and we were at Elijah's house. Elijah then said something to me like: "There’s an outdoor worship service tonight (called "Culto all'aperto" in Italian), do you want to go?"
I was a little hesitant and afraid Elijah was leading me into a cult.
This is because in Italy, most people grew up in a Catholic environment where only the Mass (called "Messa" in Italian) exists, while the word "Culto" is often associated with rituals or demonic worship by cults/sects far from God. In reality, however, the word "Culto" by itself simply means "worship of someone".
I don't remember exactly what prompted me, but I accepted (perhaps out of curiosity).

Elijah gave me a gift: he took a Bible from a shelf in his room and gave it to me. Then he said something like: "There’s a highlighted verse in this Bible", and showed it to me (Luke 12:32).

That verse was liberating for me. During those years, while the Lord had been sending me the number 23 to comfort me and tell me "Nothing is left to chance; everything is in My hands", over time, the thought had begun to creep into my mind that 32 (which is 23 with its digits reversed) might be God’s way of telling me, "You are doing something wrong." That thought filled me with panic and anxiety, but God, through Elijah, gave it a new meaning, bringing me a glorious liberation.

Below you can find some photos of the Bible that Elijah gave me that day.

We went to the outdoor worship service with one of his friends I had recently met (fictional name: Daniel).
While we were in the car, we talked at one point about the worship tapes, and I said something like: "Yeah, yesterday we listened to one recorded in the ‘Gargano’" (which is the name of a subregion of Italy).
Daniel said something like: "Oh! What a coincidence, ‘Gargano’ is also the surname of our pastor. He's going to preach at the service."
That triggered something in me, because God had been speaking to me through coincidences for years, and I thought something like: «Lord, if You have something important to do, do it tonight.»

When we arrived at the place, a square in Turin called "Piazza Santa Rita da Cascia", some brothers in faith were already setting up the instruments and microphones and preparing the seats for the worship service. All this time, my constant zoning out kept me from fully relaxing.

Later, we (Elijah and I) sat in the second row. I was specifically in the second chair from the right, and Elijah was in the third. He was to my left.
Daniel, instead, sat in the row ahead of us, in the first chair from the right. No one was in front of me, and that seat was empty.

The worship service started with some hymns, and my zoning out began to become more intense than anything I had felt before.
I couldn't maintain my concentration, not even by looking at the sky or moving my gaze from one person to another... I simply couldn't.
So I thought, «Lord, I'm going to close my eyes... I don't know the intentions of these people, but I know You can protect me even in the worst places.» (He had already done it at a rave party some time earlier).

Meanwhile, I heard people saying "Glory to God!", "Thank You, Jesus!", and "Hallelujah!" out loud, and this held me back because I thought with contempt «Ah, my Lord! These people praise You with their mouths; they are like the Pharisees who just want to show off.»

After a few songs, a boy went to the microphone and gave a testimony: he said that Jesus had spoken to him through the radio. I started crying my eyes out because I remembered how once, in the midst of my doubts and worries, I had asked the Lord a question, and when I turned on the television, the host of that program said something that answered my concern. God had used him to answer me.

While I was crying, still with my eyes closed, I began mentally thanking the Lord for all the 23s He had brought me over the years, for His closeness, and even for the quality cannabis He had allowed me to smoke (even though it wasn't something positive, I felt the need to thank Him for the privilege).
In those moments, I started feeling that presence/force that had almost crushed me in my room: from my fingertips, from my toes, and in my teeth, gradually and slowly expanding to my whole body.

I was speechless, and at one point Elijah said "Phil, are you okay?" and I said, "I feel it, Eli, I feel it!" while keeping my eyes closed.

I had the Bible Elijah had given me in my right hand, and after some time, the presence/force became so strong that I felt paralyzed by it. Especially my hands became more contracted than the rest of my body, and they began to claw under that presence/force.
The Bible was held by three fingers (thumb, index, and middle finger), and those three fingers were contracted more than the others.
I was stunned, my mouth hanging open.

I don't remember exactly what happened in that moment, but then the pastor came to the microphone and started the sermon.
In an instant, that force became so strong that I felt my whole body blocked.

My breathing, unlike years ago when I was in my room, began to increase, as if someone were blowing directly into my lungs. I felt something was about to happen, and at a certain point my chest and arms opened "of their own accord" (still permeated by that strong presence/force). At the same time, my head tilted upward, and my tongue began to shout "Thank You, Lord, THANK YOU!!" (Acts 2:26-27).
I asked myself, "Why is my tongue speaking by itself?" I wasn't sure whether what I was experiencing was really happening, so I opened my right eye a little and saw the tops of the buildings in that square. At that point, I was sure I was not dreaming or going crazy.

Full of thoughts, the rejoicing was abruptly interrupted. My head began to tilt to the right, and I felt someone standing behind me hug me and rest my head on their stomach.

My eyes were still closed, and I had a strange sensation: I felt as if my mind (or my eyes) were seeing another place, even though I was seeing pitch black and knew perfectly well that I was in Turin.

After a while, in a lit place, but without any visible light source, I began to see Elijah's face in front of me, like when you stare at the sun for a while, close your eyes, and see a flash of its image. But that flash did not disappear, and it was not static: it was colorful and moving. Elijah began to smile at me, and it was truly his smile. Then I saw him becoming emotional, and the lower parts of his eyes were red with tears. He did not speak, but it was as if I could feel his happiness, his joy. Meanwhile, I shifted my gaze (in the vision) first to the left, then back to the right multiple times because I couldn't believe it. I saw Elijah's face (with all its details, including his hair) for a few minutes, I think.
I was speechless and felt an absolute peace inside me.

Then, in the distance, to the left, I saw a bald man with glasses for a few seconds, smiling toward me. Then I began to see many faces, one after another, superimposed over Elijah's face, in his position, and disappearing after a while. I don't know how many people I saw, but at a certain point I began to see more and more faces in a shorter and shorter period of time. Then the vision ended, and the presence/force was gone. (what happened is written in Joel 2:28)

I opened my eyes, still with tears streaming down my face, and saw many people in front of me looking at me, and a man kneeling to my right, holding my right hand. I wanted to say, "What are you doing?! Look, you have to kneel before Jesus, not me. I am an ordinary man", but all I said, in a childlike voice, was, "What's your name?" and he answered with his name.

Then the man who had been hugging me came forward from the left. I turned, saw his face, and something struck me: perhaps he was the bald man with glasses I had seen in the vision. And as soon as I asked, "What's your name..." he replied, "Angelo!" (which means angel in Italian). Once again, I was speechless.

Angelo lifted me from the chair, and when he lifted me, I felt the same dizziness I had felt when I smoked my first cigarettes about 10 years earlier.

When I returned home from Turin, I noticed that cigarette smoke smelled awful to me, as it would to someone who had never smoked in their life, and I could smell it strongly from meters away. I never smoked a cigarette again, thanks to Jesus, and I never even wanted to.

A lot more happened during those days in Turin, but the story would become too long, much longer than what I have already written.
